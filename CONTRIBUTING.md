# Adding a study

## Steps

1. Create the folder `research/short-slug/`. The slug is short, in English, lowercase, words separated by hyphens: `black-win-rate`.
2. Write the study in all three languages: `README.md` (English), `README.ru.md`, `README.uk.md`. The versions must match in content: the same sections, numbers, tables and figures.
3. If the study needs other files (data, figures, code), put them in the same folder.
4. Add a row to the "Studies" table in all three root READMEs.

## Requirements for a study

- **Authors.** The authors are named at the beginning of the study.
- **Question.** It is clear what the study is about and what question it answers.
- **Data.** It is stated what data the study is based on and where it comes from. Player data that is not already public is anonymised.
- **Method.** It is clear how the results were obtained.
- **Results, conclusions and limitations.** What was found, what follows from it and what does not.

## Updating a study

Corrections to a published study are made in place. If the conclusions change substantially, publish a new study and link to the old one.

## Languages

The library is kept in English, Russian and Ukrainian. Terms are used consistently across studies.
